# Flipkart Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Flipkart e-commerce sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

An interactive dashboard was developed to monitor sales performance, identify trends, analyze product categories, and understand business performance through data visualization.

The objective is to support data-driven decision-making by presenting important business metrics in a clear and interactive format.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track important business KPIs.
- Identify sales trends and patterns.
- Analyze product and category performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior (where supported by the dataset).
- Generate meaningful insights to support business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Excel / CSV** – Dataset handling (as applicable).

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards.
- Sales performance analysis.
- Revenue and order analysis.
- Product and category-wise analysis.
- Trend analysis.
- Interactive charts, filters and slicers.
- Business performance overview.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes relevant business metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Avg Sales
- Avg Rating
- Quantity Sold

## Key Insights

The dashboard helps explore:

- Which product categories contribute most to sales?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- Which products perform better?
- What patterns can be identified in order performance?
- Which areas may offer opportunities for business improvement?

## How to Explore the Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Download and install Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Open the `.pbix` file.
4. Explore the interactive dashboard.
5. Use filters and slicers to analyze different business dimensions.
